MORE THAN A SAREE

Made slowly.
Worn forever.

Sambalpuri ikat is a conversation between the weaver and the thread. Each yarn is tie-dyed before it reaches the loom, so the pattern appears as if by magic as the cloth takes shape.

We work with Odisha’s weaving communities to bring you pieces made with care, respect, and a deep love for the craft.
